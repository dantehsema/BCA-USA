Our Foundation

Three Pillars of BCA-USA

Everything we do is rooted in culture, community, and the pursuit of a better future for the Bali Nyonga people at home and abroad.

🏛️

Cultural Preservation

We safeguard the rich traditions, language, and identity of the Bali Nyonga people, ensuring our heritage thrives for generations to come.

🤝

Community Building

Through 14 local chapters, we foster deep connections among Bali people across the United States — creating a true home away from home.

🌍

Development & Impact

We fund healthcare, education, and social welfare projects in Bali, Cameroon — turning cultural pride into lasting, tangible change.

Who We Are

A Legacy of Culture & Unity

The Bali Cultural Association USA (BCA-USA) is an apolitical, nonprofit 501(c)(3) organization founded in 1988 to serve the Bali Nyonga people of the North West Region of Cameroon residing in the United States.

With 14 active chapters from coast to coast and a National Executive Committee led by President Ni Colins Doh, BCA-USA stands as the premier cultural organization uniting Bali people across America.
