Our Story

Started in '88. Still Building.

BCA-USA launched in 1988 when the diaspora needed one organized voice — to protect the culture and serve the community on US soil.

Today: 14 active chapters, a National Executive Committee, 12+ standing committees, and members from the East Coast to the Pacific.

501(c)(3). Apolitical. Driven by cultural pride, unity, and impact — here and in Bali, Cameroon.

Purpose

Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

Preserve. Promote. Push Forward.

Protect Bali Nyonga culture, strengthen ties across the diaspora, and fund healthcare, education, and welfare back home — with clarity and accountability.

🔭 Our Vision

One BCA. One Story. One Voice.

A visible, connected organization that amplifies every member — keeping Bali Nyonga heritage strong in the US and on the global stage.
