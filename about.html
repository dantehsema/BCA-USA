Our Story

A Legacy Born in 1988

The Bali Cultural Association USA (BCA-USA) was founded in 1988 by members of the Bali Nyonga diaspora who recognized the need for a unified, organized body to preserve their culture and serve their community in the United States.

From its humble beginnings, BCA-USA has grown into a nationwide organization with 14 active chapters, a National Executive Committee, more than 12 standing committees, and a passionate membership spread from the East Coast to the Pacific.

Registered as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, BCA-USA operates as an apolitical organization — guided solely by cultural pride, fraternal unity, and the mission to uplift lives both here in America and back home in Bali, Cameroon.

Purpose

Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

Preserve. Promote. Propagate.

To preserve, promote, and propagate the culture of the Bali Nyonga people — fostering fraternal relations among all people of Bali Chamba heritage and friends of Bali, while improving the quality of life in Bali through development projects in healthcare, education, and social welfare.

🔭 Our Vision

One BCA. One Story. One Voice.

A connected, visible, and globally recognized cultural organization that communicates transparently, amplifies member voices, and ensures the rich heritage of Bali Nyonga thrives for generations to come — both in the United States and around the world.

How We're Organized

Organizational Structure

BCA-USA operates through a layered governance structure ensuring both national coherence and local chapter autonomy.

🏛️

Board of Directors

Provides high-level oversight, strategic direction, and governance for the national association.

⚙️

National Executive Committee

Manages day-to-day operations, implements BOD strategy, and coordinates national programs.

📋

12+ Standing Committees

Specialized committees covering culture, finance, youth, development, communications, and more.

🗺️

14 US Chapters

Local chapters across the nation, each with its own leadership, hosting local events and chapter meetings.
