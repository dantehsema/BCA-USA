Why Join

Benefits of Membership

As a BCA-USA member you'll gain access to a network of over 14 chapters, exclusive events, and the chance to directly support development back home in Bali.

🤝

Community Access

Connect with Bali Nyonga people across the US through chapter meetings, national events, and digital networks.

🎉

Exclusive Events

Members get priority access and discounted tickets to the Annual Convention, Leadership Summit, Cultural Night Gala, and more.

🌍

Development Impact

Your dues directly fund healthcare, education, and infrastructure projects in Bali, Cameroon.

🏆

Recognition & Voice

Participate in BCA-USA governance, vote in elections, and have a seat at the table in shaping our organization's future.

📚

Cultural Resources

Access to cultural education materials, language resources, and heritage preservation programs for you and your family.

🔗

National Network

A direct connection to BCA-USA's 14 chapters — your community wherever you are in the United States.
